pairs a clear offer with a working example; documentation chrome begins on
articles, leaving the homepage free of a permanent sidebar.

OWN-WORLD: Near-white, charcoal, violet actions, lavender space, sage event

also serves as favicon. Zaidan owns controls, links and interactive states.

lavender stage holds event, source and functional counter notes, with visible
controls. Signature interaction: dispatch renews a short event-note pulse and
updates state, derivation and journal. Reduced motion retains all updates.
